# Digest — D2 optics & print — round 1 (accessed 2026-11-05)
- DVD track pitch 0.74 µm ≈ 1350 lines/mm vs CD 1.6 µm ≈ 625 l/mm; a DVD spectroscope resolves Hg 577/579 nm, CD cannot; dual-layer DVDs and DVD-RAM unsuitable. Source: Wakabayashi-style "A DVD Spectroscope" J Chem Educ 2006 (sciencemadness mirror). conf high.
- Grating equation mλ = d(sinα + sinβ); 1000 l/mm lab film d=1000 nm. inpharmix.com. conf high (textbook).
- Black PLA/PETG/ABS/PA at 1 mm: ~0% transmittance; at 2 mm effectively zero all wavelengths; black PC and TPU transmit in IR; white/light colours transmit up to ~19–31% at 4 mm. Source: "Optical transmittance of 3D printing materials", NSF PAR 10298258. conf high.
- Some black PLA brands leak at 689/828 nm (PLOS One 2021, 10.1371/journal.pone.0253181). conf high.
- Practitioners: matte black PLA, 0.16–0.2 mm layers, 100% infill for fiber spectrometer (GaudiLabs); 0.2 mm, 30% infill, supports, black PLA, tape over gaps (Steve's Open Lab 2025-08-21); film gratings in card slide mounts warp → curved lines; sandwich between glass slides. conf medium.

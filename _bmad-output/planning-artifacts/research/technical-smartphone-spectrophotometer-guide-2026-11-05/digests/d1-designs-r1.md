# Digest — D1 existing designs — round 1 (accessed 2026-11-05)
- Winters et al., "3D-Printable and open-source modular smartphone visible spectrophotometer", HardwareX 10 (2021) e00232, DOI 10.1016/j.ohx.2021.e00232, Bethel University; published online 2021-10-08. Article CC BY 4.0; hardware spec table states CC BY-SA 4.0; files at https://osf.io/7ewb3 (registration). Separate OSF project osf.io/ptbuc labelled CC-BY 4.0. Source: hardware-x.com fulltext. conf high.
- Same: double-beam visible spectrophotometer; cost $25.53 spectrophotometer, $175.73 sources (flashlight, laser, penlights), $64.26 ELP USB camera. Designed for low-end consumer printers and hardware-store parts; Imperial units with mm in parentheses. conf high.
- Same: parts DBS01..DBS25 (slit tube, sample holder, grating mount, tube adapter, source adapter, block, cuvette cover, fluorescence cover, plug, side cover, LED adapter, ... iPhone mount DBS15, center camera phone mount DBS16, ELP camera mount DBS17, phone clamp DBS18, knob DBS19, USB cam base/lid DBS20/21, slit mount DBS22, slit wedge DBS23, flashlight tube DBS24, blank mounting plate DBS25). STL+STEP; BOM xlsx on OSF; original CAD on Onshape. conf high (OSF API listing + article).
- Same: iPhone mount fits SE,6,6s,7,8,X; center-camera mount for phones with centered camera; blank plate to solvent-weld any phone case. Modularity lets one part (e.g. flashlight tube) be redesigned alone. conf high.
- Smith Lab SpecPhone (Grasse, Torcasio, Smith, J Chem Educ 2016 93(1):146, DOI 10.1021/acs.jchemed.5b00654) and DualSpec (Bogucki et al., J Chem Educ 2019 96(7):1527, DOI 10.1021/acs.jchemed.8b00870): standard 1 cm polystyrene cuvette, 1000 lines/mm film grating (~$2.50 sheet), 1"x1" mirror at 45°, removable slits 0.1–5 mm; STL on Thingiverse thing:3404762; dual beam improves S/N. Source adamsmithlab.org/specphone. conf high.
- Bruininks & Juurlink, J Chem Educ 2022 99(5):2168 (Leiden): periscope type, 3 printed parts + 1/8 DVD wedge + 2 razor blades + mirror; attaches phone's flexible back cover so one calibration persists; optional module with PMMA semimicro cuvette, white+UV LED, 9V; emission/absorption/fluorescence; < $40. Printables model 1355560 (updated 2025-07-15). conf high.
- Cokley et al., J Chem Educ 2024 101(7):2665: 3D-printable modular absorption spectrophotometer + fluorimeter (orientation change). Paywalled; cited by 12 incl. Nguyen et al. Applied Spectroscopy Practica 2025 (spectroscope-inspired printed smartphone spectrophotometer), Pap & Stratton J Chem Educ 2024 101(12) (universal smartphone fluorescence). conf medium (abstract only).
- Public Lab: papercraft/foldable (~$10) and desktop kits, CERN OHL 1.1/1.2; OSHWA cert US000089; designed for intro use, "not meant for quantitative research"; ~400–700 nm, better than 3 nm/pixel possible. Spectral Workbench website offline for lack of funds; static V1/V2 exist; spectral-workbench.js GPL-3.0, 47 open issues. conf high.
- Printables community: many single-phone designs (CMF Phone 2 Pro), Tiny Pocket Spectrometer (DVD grating, interchangeable slits, "a toy, not a scientific instrument", pairs with web app spectro-web). conf medium.
